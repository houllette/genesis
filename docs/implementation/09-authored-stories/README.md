## Outcome and scope

A GM authors a three-beat situation, previews different characters/companions,
publishes its discovery and duration rules, and logs off. A provider-free StoryRun
resolves in an Experience through actual choices and can mark it ready on a
validated terminal state. World incorporation still follows 08's policy/review.
The native GM editor is the priority; player transport comes in 11.

Proposed homes: Genesis.Content, pure story/predicate/group policy, durable
StoryRun orchestration and native editor/preview views. Use validated data, not
macros or a general-purpose workflow framework.

## TDD slices

1. **Definition and duration.** Version IDs, purpose/tone, discovery predicates,
   role/entity bindings, stakes, constraints, capabilities, qualities, optional
   approaches and bounded follow-ups. Define per-beat/local duration and terminal
   elapsed-time calculation consistent with 08. Reject broken references,
   unsupported mechanics, invalid timing and unbounded cycles.
2. **Context and preview.** Declare variant priority/compatibility and permitted
   changes to participants, approaches, stakes, costs and follow-ups. Preview
   ordinary visitor, prior rescuer and allied/hostile companions using actual
   sourced facts and a bounded rehearsal. Show why a variant applies without
   revealing private context in a player preview. Rehearsal cannot export rewards.
3. **Outcomes beyond a menu.** Support available/active/completed/failed/bypassed/
   invalidated/expired/abandoned states. Implement a bounded real bargain, refusal
   and departure through engine intents. Confirmation binds costs and revisions;
   compound actions preserve partial success. Suggested paths are not mandatory.
   Combat remains explicitly unsupported until the pilot supplies it in 12.
4. **Atomic progress.** Commit story cursor, experience outcome/receipt and
   durable follow-up together. Revalidate changed roster/dependencies; a dead NPC
   or exhausted market cannot be reset by a beat. Enforce unique opportunities
   and reward claims. Abandonment preserves local consequences for reconciliation.
5. **Solo, sync and async policy.** Persist participants, scoped choices/votes,
   waits, tie/default and pause/resume rules. Joining/revocation never grants
   another actor's control. A paused Experience suspends configured deadlines;
   real-time meeting schedules do not determine fictional duration.
6. **Narrative completion.** A published terminal predicate seals actual outcomes
   and elapsed time through 08's engine entrypoint under a GM-delegated policy.
   Race GM finish, terminal finish and retry: one manifest. No model decides
   completion or duration through free prose. Default incorporation requires GM
   review; optional auto-incorporation requires every admitted run ready and no
   conflict/review-required effects. An offline GM need not block story completion.
7. **Native authoring workflow.** Draft/edit/validate/preview/publish/retire in
   existing authenticated routes. Show dependency diagnostics, duration, variant
   examples and Published versus Working state; a definition never executes its
   future events. Support an unstructured GM-led experience without a StoryRun.
8. **Ashfall proof.** Negotiate instead of the suggested confrontation, refuse,
   and abandon after expenditure. Pause/restart; complete and incorporate; a later
   campaign discovers a changed resource/relationship without secret dialogue.
   Include a minor and a major context variant plus irrelevant-context controls.

## Handoff criteria

- [ ] GM creates/previews/publishes useful beats and duration rules without code.
- [ ] Off-menu supported actions and genuine failure do not force a planned ending.
- [ ] Story cursor/outcomes are atomic in the Experience, not prematurely canonical.
- [ ] Solo/sync/async decisions and paused deadlines recover without a connected host.
- [ ] Terminal completion and GM completion are authorized, consistent and idempotent.
- [ ] Claims, rehearsal isolation and incorporation prevent duplicate rewards/facts.
- [ ] Native editor and player-view preview preserve knowledge and explain failures.
- [ ] mix precommit passes; [handoff.md](handoff.md) records predicates, timing,
  actual routes/APIs and the next fresh agent's exact acceptance commands.
